1. Dynamic Screener Strategy
Universe Selection & Volatility Ranking
Screen Logic
Replaces static watchlist with periodic automated screening of the configured moomoo market (US, HK, SG or CN). Filters for liquid instruments and ranks survivors by swing volatility and trend strength.
Core Mathematical & Filtering Logic
  • Stage 1 (Pre-filter): Filters universe by minimum price, maximum price, and minimum average volume using batched briefs (or historical bars for US stocks).
  • Stage 2 (ATR% Volatility): Computes Average True Range over period 14 normalized by price: ATR% = (ATR_14 / Close) * 100. Ranks candidates descending.
  • Trend Directional Strength: Measures exponential moving average gap: (EMA_20 - EMA_50) / EMA_50 * 100. Positive values signify sustained uptrends.
  • Top-N Selection: Retains only the top N highest-ranked volatility candidates for active trading.

2. Candlestick Pattern Engine
Deterministic Price Action Setup Detection
Trigger Logic
Evaluates closed multi-hour/daily candlestick bars for classic institutional price action patterns. Signals an initial directional bias (BUY or SELL) with assigned pattern strength.
Supported Candlestick Formations
  • Inside Bar (Strength 0.45): Current bar's High and Low are completely contained within the previous bar's range. Signifies consolidation prior to continuation breakout.
  • Bullish/Bearish Engulfing (Strength 0.60): Current bar body completely engulfs prior bar body against previous trend, signaling strong momentum shift.
  • Hammer & Inverted Hammer (Strength 0.55): Small body with a lower/upper shadow at least twice the body size, showing sharp price rejection.
  • Morning Star / Evening Star (Strength 0.70): 3-candle reversal pattern consisting of a large directional bar, an indecision star, and a confirming reversal bar.

• Add Fair Value Gaps (FVG): Add a 3-bar imbalance detector in src/patterns.py where Candle 1 High < Candle 3 Low (bullish imbalance).

• Multi-Timeframe Trend Confirmation: Only allow BUY signals if price is trading above the 200 EMA on the Daily timeframe.
3. Kronos AI Foundation Model
Offline K-Line Generative Trajectory Filter
AI Confirmation
Offline K-Line foundation model (AAAI 2026). Ingests normalized historical price action tokens, generates forward trajectory paths, and validates whether the statistical probability supports the detected pattern.
Generative Inference & Validation Logic
  • Temporal Tokenization: Quantizes normalized OHLCV bars into temporal discrete tokens for offline context understanding.
  • Autoregressive Prediction: Generates synthetic future candlestick paths over the designated forecast_horizon (default 6 bars).
  • Monte Carlo Ensemble: Generates sample_count forward paths with temperature sampling (0.5) to quantify uncertainty and direction.
  • Confidence Decision Gate: Calculates directional agreement percentage: conf = agreement_count / total_samples. Confirms trade only if conf >= threshold (62%).

4. Exchange Execution & Order Routing
Exchange Hours Compliance & Resilient Routing
Execution Logic
Dispatches orders through moomoo OpenD (OpenSecTradeContext) with session validation for US, HK, SGX and A-share markets, preventing off-market-hours exchange rejections and handling bracket protections.
Session-Aware Execution Mechanics
  • Limit Order Placement (Default): Places entries using order_type: "LIMIT" priced at the latest completed bar close, safely queued by the broker even outside regular trading hours.
  • Off-Hours Auto-Fallback: MARKET orders are only sent while the exchange session is open; outside it the entry is placed as a LIMIT at the last close instead.
  • Protective Order Fallback: If the broker rejects separate resting stop orders before the buy order fills (e.g. short-selling not permitted on cash/paper accounts), software trailing stop safely assumes position control.
  • Code Normalization & Ticks: Bare tickers get the moomoo market prefix (AAPL ➔ US.AAPL, 00700 ➔ HK.00700) and prices are rounded to each exchange's tick table (incl. the HKEX spread table).
  • Broker-side Protection: Stop (OrderType.STOP) and target (LIMIT) exits are placed GTC once the entry fills, so they keep protecting the position even if the agent is offline.

5. Capital Guard & Position Limits
Hard Pre-Trade Portfolio Risk Checks
Risk Logic
Acts as the primary protective firewall before any order can be dispatched. Restricts portfolio exposure, halts trading if daily drawdown thresholds are reached, and enforces cooling intervals.
Risk Enforcement Checks
  • Daily Cumulative Loss Cap: Tracks both realized P&L and floating unrealized loss. If cumulative daily loss exceeds max_daily_loss, all new entries are blocked.
  • Max Concurrent Open Positions: Limits total active swing positions across all symbols to prevent capital overcommitment.
  • Symbol Trade Cooldown: Enforces min_minutes_between_trades (e.g. 240m / 4h) on any individual ticker to avoid chasing consecutive whipsaws.
  • Asymmetric Bracket Distances: Defines predefined stop-loss and take-profit distances ensuring positive risk-to-reward ratios (e.g. 1:2 R:R).

6. Chandelier Trailing Stop & Exits
Dynamic Profit Locking & High-Water Ratchet
Exit Logic
Independent fast-cadence monitoring engine running every 20 seconds. Tracks favorable price excursions and locks in profits by ratcheting the protective stop behind price peaks.
Trailing Stop Mechanics
  • High-Water / Low-Water Tracking: Maintains the peak favorable price achieved since position entry (high_water for BUYs, low_water for SELLs).
  • Profit Trigger Delay: Trailing does not ratchet on initial entry wiggles; only activates after price moves at least trigger_points (e.g. 0.10) in profit.
  • One-Way Ratchet: The stop trails trail_distance behind peak price and only ratchets in the favorable direction—never loosening.
  • Automated Market Close: As soon as current price breaches the ratcheted stop or reaches the profit target, dispatches an automated market close order.
