    1. Brokers, markets and sessions

    Where it trades and when
    Step 1

    One process runs every enabled broker side by side. Each has its own connection, trade log, risk limits, screener and exchange calendar. All of them share the one offline Kronos model.

    How it works
    • Brokers: moomoo (US, HK, SG, CN), Tiger, and experimental Zerodha, Angel One and IG adapters. Paper trading where the broker offers it.
    • A broker can trade while another supplies its prices (data_source). Example: Zerodha's free API has no market data, so prices come from Angel One.
    • The agent works only while the exchange is open, with holidays and early closes from its calendar. It warms up before the open and sleeps after the close, checking positions.
    • Only closed candles are used; the bar still forming is ignored.

    2. Screener

    Which symbols get watched
    Step 2

    Picks the symbols the agent watches, on a timer. With the screener off, it watches the fixed symbols: list instead.

    How it works
    • Starts from your candidate list, plus moomoo Market Movers lists if switched on (top gainers, top losers, hot list, 5-minute movers, short selling).
    • Keeps symbols inside your price range with enough average volume.
    • Keeps a volatility band: ATR as % of price, between your minimum and maximum.
    • Ranks by ATR% or, with the stock score on, by relative strength against the market, relative volume and how tight the Bollinger bands are. The top N are watched.
    • The screener only chooses what to watch. Every trade still has to pass steps 3 to 7.

    3. Candlestick patterns

    The trigger
    Step 3

    When a new candle closes, the agent checks it against four patterns. A pattern gives a direction, buy or sell, and a strength. If several fire on the same candle, the strongest one goes forward.

    The four patterns
    • Engulfing (strength 0.7): the candle's body swallows the previous opposite-coloured body. Bullish = buy, bearish = sell.
    • Hammer (0.6, buy): small body near the top, lower wick at least twice the body.
    • Shooting star (0.6, sell): small body near the bottom, upper wick at least twice the body.
    • Inside bar (0.45): the candle sits inside the previous one's range; direction follows the previous candle.

    4. Indicator check

    Drops setups that fight the trend
    Step 4

    Before asking Kronos, the agent reads the usual indicators and rejects signals that go against them. This check can only stop a signal; it never creates one.

    How it works
    • Reads EMA 9/20/50/200, ADX, Supertrend, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, Bollinger, Donchian, volume/OBV and, intraday, session VWAP.
    • Turns them into a bias score from −100 to +100 toward the trade; the signal needs at least your minimum.
    • Optional: buy only when the fast moving average is above the slow one (and the reverse for sells).
    • Skips buys when RSI is overbought and sells when it is oversold.
    • With too few candles, the check is skipped and the signal passes.

    5. Your rules (skills.md)

    Your own price-action habits, in plain words
    Step 5

    Rules you write yourself in a Markdown file, such as "don't chase far above VWAP" or "inside bars need a trend". Like the indicator check, a rule can only stop a signal; it never creates one or changes what Kronos sees.

    How it works
    • Each rule names the side it applies to (and optionally which patterns), with block if and only if conditions on measures such as VWAP distance, RSI, ADX, volume, gap and time of day.
    • A matching rule stops the signal, and its name appears in the decision log.
    • A rule that can't be checked right now, such as VWAP on daily candles, lets the signal pass. A rule the agent can't read is listed as an error and skipped.
    • "Log" mode only records what the rules would have stopped, so you can try a rule safely first.
    • Edits are picked up without a restart.

    6. Kronos confirmation

    The offline forecasting model
    Step 6

    Kronos is a model trained on candlestick charts. It runs on this computer with no internet. It forecasts the next few candles, and the trade goes ahead only if enough of them move the signal's way.

    How it works
    • Reads the latest closed candles (up to the model's context length) and forecasts the next horizon candles.
    • It samples several forecasts and averages them into one path.
    • Confidence is the share of forecast candles that close above the signal candle for a buy, or below it for a sell.
    • The signal passes only above 50% and at or above your threshold.
    • A shadow model, if set, is asked the same question in the background. Only its answer is recorded (card 11).

    7. Risk rules

    The last word before an order
    Step 7

    Sets the stop, the target and the size, then checks the account limits. If any rule fails, no order is sent. Exits are never blocked.

    How it works
    • Stop and target: fixed price distance, % of price, or a multiple of ATR (target as a multiple of the stop, R).
    • Size: fixed quantity, or what risks a set amount at the stop, capped by the account limits and rounded to the lot size.
    • Blocks the trade at the daily loss cap, at the open-position limit, during a symbol's cooldown, past the daily trade limit, outside the entry window, below the minimum reward:risk, or for blocked symbols.
    • Never trades a symbol you already hold yourself.

    8. Orders and exits at the broker

    Getting in, with protection
    Step 8

    Sends the entry and, once it fills, places the stop and target at the broker, so they protect the position even if the agent stops.

    How it works
    • Entries are day orders: a limit at the signal candle's close, or a market order while the session is open.
    • An entry still unfilled after the timeout is cancelled.
    • Exits: stop and target orders. These are GTC on live moomoo and Tiger accounts, re-placed daily on moomoo paper accounts, and GTT triggers on Zerodha. Angel One gets limit-priced orders, because SEBI rules don't allow market orders from algorithms.
    • Prices are rounded to each exchange's tick size, and quantities to its lot size.
    • Manual orders from the Positions tab skip steps 3 to 7 but get the same stop, target and trailing.

    9. Managing open trades

    Trailing, closing, and your own holdings
    Step 9

    A separate, faster loop watches the agent's open trades. It moves stops up, closes trades on time and leaves your own holdings alone.

    How it works
    • Trailing stop: once a trade is far enough in profit, the stop follows the best price at a set distance (or a multiple of ATR), and only ever tightens.
    • Intraday mode: every agent trade is closed a set number of minutes before the close, win or lose, and anything left from yesterday is closed at the open.
    • Your holdings: positions the agent didn't open are shown but never sold or traded by it.
    • A position the broker no longer holds is marked closed instead of being watched forever.

    10. Self-learning (fine-tuning Kronos)

    Teaching Kronos your markets
    After the close

    Trains a copy of Kronos on candles from your own markets, using the method from the Kronos repository. It then compares the copy with the original on recent candles neither has seen. The trading model only changes when you choose a new one.

    The steps, in order
    1. Collect: at each market close, saves the candles of every symbol the agent screened, decided on or traded, on every broker.
    2. Train: starts from the original Kronos every time, so runs never stack. A share of each batch (30% by default) ends exactly where the agent asked Kronos for a verdict, winners and losers alike. Run it here on the CPU, on a schedule, or on a GPU computer with the exported kit.
    3. Compare: the newest candles (15% by default) are held back. Both models forecast them, and the run gets a verdict: better, no gain or worse. Only "better" models are kept.
    4. Shadow test (card 11), then Use this model and restart. "Back to original" undoes it.
    5. Save version (card 12) to keep it for later.

    11. Shadow test

    Try a new model on live signals, risk-free
    Alongside step 6

    A fine-tuned model sits next to the trading model and answers the same question on every live signal. Its answers are only recorded, so it can never place or block a trade.

    How it works
    • Runs in the background after the trading model has answered, so it never delays a decision.
    • Records both confidences. Once later candles are in, it records which model was right about the direction.
    • The Self-learning card shows how often the two agreed and who was right when they didn't.
    • A shadow model that fails to load is logged and ignored.

    12. Saved model versions

    Keep good models and get them back any time
    After the close

    Training runs on this computer are cleaned up over time. Save as version keeps a model as a GitHub Release on your private repository, with its scores and your note, and Download brings it back.

    How it works
    • Each version is a release tagged model-v1, model-v2, …, holding the model's files. It is never marked as the code's latest release.
    • Download checks the file's checksum, then lists the model under Training runs, ready for Shadow test or Use this model.
    • Saving or downloading never changes the model the agent trades with.

    13. Watching it work

    Records, alerts and display-only tools
    Always on

    Everything the agent decides is written down, and you can follow it from the dashboard, your phone or Telegram. The tools marked display-only never influence a trade.

    What's there
    • Trade log: every signal, verdict, order and result, in one database per broker. See Decisions & Audit Log and Performance.
    • Telegram: trades, rejections, errors and market open/close messages.
    • Command Center: today's P&L, the latest Kronos verdict with its forecast, what the agent did today, and live activity.
    • Display only: the market sentiment meter, support/resistance lines on the chart, and the stock score in "log" mode.
    • Heartbeat: "Not reporting" appears when the agent hasn't checked in for 3 minutes.
